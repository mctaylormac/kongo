Privacy Policy

This Privacy Notice for kongo describes how and why we access, collect, store, use, and share personal information when you use our services.

Résumé des points clés

Kongo est une plateforme numérique de réservation de transport interurbain qui met en relation voyageurs et agences de transport via une plateforme web, applications mobiles et bornes de réservation.

1. What information do we collect?

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express interest in obtaining information, participate in activities, or contact us.

The personal information collected may include names, phone numbers, email addresses, usernames, passwords, contact preferences, billing addresses, and payment data when you purchase services.

We do not process sensitive information. Payment data is handled by third-party payment providers, and Kongo does not directly store sensitive banking information.

Application data may include geolocation information, mobile device access permissions, device identifiers, operating system details, and technical usage information needed for security, troubleshooting, analytics, and internal reporting.

2. How do we process your information?

We process your personal information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, maintain security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

  • Facilitate account creation and authentication
  • Deliver and manage transport services
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send administrative information and updates
  • Manage orders, payments, and exchanges
  • Request feedback
  • Send marketing communications when permitted
  • Protect our Services and detect fraud
  • Evaluate and improve our products and user experience

3. When and with whom do we share your personal information?

We may share your information with third-party vendors, service providers, consultants, affiliates, business partners, and in connection with business transfers or acquisitions.

The categories of third parties may include data analytics services, cloud computing services, data storage providers, payment processors, performance monitoring tools, authentication services, and website hosting providers.

When we use Google Maps Platform APIs, we may share information needed for route display, bus localization, GPS tracking, trip-related information, and similar location-based services. Google Maps may use GPS, Wi-Fi, and cell towers to estimate your location.

4. Do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies, including web beacons and pixels, to maintain security, improve site functions, store preferences, and provide analytics and advertising services.

We may share information with Google Analytics to understand how the Services are used. You can opt out of tracking across Services by visiting the Google Analytics opt-out page.

5. How long do we keep your information?

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

When there is no ongoing legitimate business need, we delete or anonymize data, or secure it in isolated storage until deletion is possible.

6. How do we keep your information safe?

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and transmission of information over the Internet remains at your own risk.

7. Do we collect information from minors?

We do not knowingly collect data from or market to minors. Kongo services are intended for individuals aged 18 and above. If a minor needs to travel, the booking must be made by a parent, legal guardian, or responsible adult in accordance with applicable transport rules.

8. What are your privacy rights?

Depending on your location, you may have the right to review, correct, delete, or update your personal information, or withdraw consent for processing.

You may also manage your account settings directly or contact us to terminate your account. We may retain some information to prevent fraud, troubleshoot issues, enforce legal terms, or comply with applicable requirements.
